Grid Intelligence is a forecasting and dispatch layer for battery storage in wholesale and industrial
energy markets. It forecasts prices at the node level, with uncertainty, and turns those forecasts into
dispatch schedules that respect power limits, state of charge and battery degradation.

**Where it stands**

- Two paying industrial clients in Europe (battery sizing, storage investment and dispatch analysis). The first converted into a performance-based dispatch pilot.
- A technical pilot at a solar-plus-storage microgrid in the US Southeast, integrated with the site's energy management system.
- $30,000 in non-dilutive funding; Greentown Labs climatetech accelerator; Duke Melissa & Doug Entrepreneurs program.
