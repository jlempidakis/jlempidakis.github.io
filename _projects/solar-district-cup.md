Our team, Duke Solar Solutions, placed 2nd in the Bring-Your-Own-District division of the
US Department of Energy's Solar District Cup, the first time Duke has competed. Teams design a
full solar and storage proposal for a real district, from conceptual design and financial analysis
to a final presentation before judges.

**What we designed**

A 1.189 MW rooftop solar array with 11.7 MWh of battery storage for the Duke Hospital Medical
District in Durham, NC.

- 1,516 MWh generated on site each year, fully self-consumed with zero backfeed to the grid (performance ratio 0.83, yield 1,275 kWh/kWp).
- 7,304 tonnes of CO2 avoided over 25 years, against 232,000 offset credits.
- A 2,069 kW outage gap covered for critical hospital load, at a 50% critical load factor.
- More than $5M in 25-year net savings, self-funding through demand-charge reduction and arbitrage, with Direct Pay making direct ownership the dominant ownership route.

**Team.** Kyle D'Souza, Josh Sadun, Bryce Outten, Felipe Jaramillo, Yiannis Lempidakis, Ella Veysel,
Kylie Gancos and Arthur Gwozdz, with external mentor Calvin Horton (DCE Solar) and support from
Casey Collins and Dean Jerome P. Lynch.
