A browser-based console that rebuilt the analytical record for a 60 m³/h closed-loop wash-water
treatment plant at Plastika Kritis.

- Found six classes of data-integrity failure that were producing phantom regulatory exceedances.
- 62 samples, 27 parameters, checked against two regulatory frameworks.
- Fed a costed investment proposal with three risk scenarios, projected to cut water-recycling and chemical costs by about 10%.
